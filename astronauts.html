👨‍🚀 Indian Astronauts 🇮🇳

Meet the Indians who have travelled beyond Earth.

👨‍🚀

Rakesh Sharma

🇮🇳 First Indian Citizen in Space

Mission: Soyuz T-11

Year: 1984

Rakesh Sharma became the first Indian citizen to travel to space on 3 April 1984.

He travelled aboard the Soviet spacecraft Soyuz T-11 and stayed in space for about eight days.

During the mission, he conducted experiments and photographed Earth from space.

👨‍🚀

Shubhanshu Shukla

🇮🇳 Second Indian Citizen in Space

Mission: Axiom Mission 4

Destination: International Space Station

Shubhanshu Shukla travelled to the International Space Station in 2025.

He flew aboard a Crew Dragon spacecraft launched by a SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket.

He conducted scientific activities and experiments during his stay aboard the space station.

🚀 India's Human Space Journey

1984 — Rakesh Sharma

First Indian citizen to travel to space aboard Soyuz T-11.

2018 — Gaganyaan Programme

India announced its human spaceflight programme, Gaganyaan, with the goal of sending Indian astronauts to low Earth orbit.

2025 — Shubhanshu Shukla

Became the second Indian citizen to travel to space, as part of Axiom Mission 4 to the International Space Station.

Future — Gaganyaan 🇮🇳

India is developing its own human-spaceflight capability, including a crewed spacecraft and launch system.
