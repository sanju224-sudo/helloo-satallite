🪐 Explore the Planets

Discover how the planets were known or discovered, what they are made of and what makes each world unique.

☿️

Mercury

Known since: Ancient times

Discovered by: No single discoverer. Mercury is visible to the naked eye and was known to ancient civilizations.

What is there? A rocky surface covered with craters, mountains and large impact basins.

Atmosphere: Mercury has an extremely thin exosphere.

Moons: 0

Special fact: It is the closest planet to the Sun.

♀️

Venus

Known since: Ancient times

Discovered by: No single discoverer. Venus is easily visible from Earth.

What is there? Rocky plains, mountains and many volcanoes.

Atmosphere: Mostly carbon dioxide with thick clouds containing sulfuric acid.

Moons: 0

Special fact: Venus is the hottest planet in the Solar System.

🌍

Earth

Known since: Prehistoric times

Discovery: Earth was not discovered as a new planet. Humans have always lived on it.

What is there? Oceans, continents, mountains, forests, atmosphere and a huge variety of life.

Atmosphere: Mainly nitrogen and oxygen.

Moon: 1

Special fact: Earth is currently the only world known to support life.

🔴

Mars

Known since: Ancient times

Discovered by: No single discoverer. Mars was observed by ancient civilizations.

What is there? Rocky terrain, volcanoes, valleys, polar ice caps and dust.

Atmosphere: Very thin, mostly carbon dioxide.

Moons: 2 — Phobos and Deimos

Special fact: Mars has Olympus Mons, the largest known volcano in the Solar System.

🟠

Jupiter

Known since: Ancient times

Discovered by: No single discoverer.

What is there? A huge atmosphere of hydrogen and helium, powerful storms and many cloud bands.

Atmosphere: Mostly hydrogen and helium.

Moons: Many known moons, including Io, Europa, Ganymede and Callisto.

Special fact: Jupiter is the largest planet.

🪐

Saturn

Known since: Ancient times

Discovered by: No single discoverer.

What is there? A gas giant surrounded by an extensive system of icy and rocky rings.

Atmosphere: Mostly hydrogen and helium.

Moons: Many known moons, including Titan and Enceladus.

Special fact: Saturn has the most prominent ring system in the Solar System.

🔵

Uranus

Discovered: 1781

Discoverer: William Herschel

What is there? An ice giant with a cold atmosphere and faint rings.

Atmosphere: Mainly hydrogen and helium with methane.

Moons: 28 known moons

Special fact: Uranus rotates on its side because of its extreme axial tilt.

🔵

Neptune

Discovered: 1846

Discovery: Neptune was predicted mathematically and then observed by astronomers.

What is there? A deep blue ice giant with powerful storms and extremely fast winds.

Atmosphere: Mainly hydrogen, helium and methane.

Moons: 16 known moons, including Triton.

Special fact: Neptune is the farthest recognized planet from the Sun.
