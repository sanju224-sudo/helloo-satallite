🌌 Our Solar System

Explore the Sun and the eight planets orbiting around it.

🪐 Planet Overview

☿ Mercury

The closest planet to the Sun and the smallest planet in our Solar System.

♀ Venus

The hottest planet in the Solar System with a very thick atmosphere.

🌍 Earth

Our home planet and the only known planet with life.

🔴 Mars

Known as the Red Planet because of iron-rich material on its surface.

🟠 Jupiter

The largest planet in the Solar System and a gas giant.

🪐 Saturn

Famous for its spectacular system of rings.

🔵 Uranus

An ice giant that rotates with an extreme axial tilt.

🔵 Neptune

The farthest planet from the Sun and a very windy world.
