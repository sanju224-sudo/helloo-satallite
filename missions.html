🌙 Indian Space Missions

Explore India's journey from Earth to the Moon, Mars and beyond.

🌙

Chandrayaan-1

Launch: 2008

Chandrayaan-1 was India's first mission to the Moon. It helped scientists study the lunar surface and provided important evidence related to water molecules on the Moon.

It was launched using India's PSLV rocket.

🌙

Chandrayaan-2

Launch: 2019

Chandrayaan-2 consisted of an orbiter, lander and rover. The lander attempted a soft landing near the lunar south pole.

The orbiter continued studying the Moon from orbit.

🌕

Chandrayaan-3

Launch: 2023

Chandrayaan-3 successfully achieved a soft landing on the Moon on 23 August 2023.

India became the fourth country to successfully soft-land on the Moon.

The mission included the Vikram lander and Pragyan rover.

🔴

Mangalyaan

Launch: 2013

Mangalyaan, also known as the Mars Orbiter Mission, was India's first interplanetary mission.

It successfully entered orbit around Mars in 2014.

The mission studied the Martian surface and atmosphere.

☀️

Aditya-L1

Launch: 2023

Aditya-L1 is India's first space-based solar observatory.

It studies the Sun, including the solar atmosphere and solar activity.

The spacecraft operates around the Sun-Earth L1 point.

👨‍🚀

Gaganyaan

Mission: Human Spaceflight

Gaganyaan is India's human spaceflight programme. Its objective is to send Indian astronauts to low Earth orbit and safely return them to Earth.

India is developing the crew module, service module, launch vehicle and other systems required for the mission.

🚀 Why Are Space Missions Important?

Space missions help scientists understand planets, stars, the Sun and our own Earth.

They also help develop technologies used in communication, navigation, weather forecasting, Earth observation and scientific research.

India's missions have helped build experience in launching satellites, exploring other worlds and developing human-spaceflight technology.
