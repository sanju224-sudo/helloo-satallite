🔭 Amazing Space Facts

Some fascinating facts about our universe.

☀️

The Sun is Huge

The Sun contains about 99.8% of the total mass of our Solar System.

🌍

Earth is Moving

Earth rotates on its axis and also travels around the Sun. We are constantly moving through space.

🌙

The Moon Has No Light of Its Own

The Moon appears bright because it reflects sunlight.

🔴

Mars is Red

Mars looks reddish because iron minerals in its surface have reacted with oxygen, creating iron oxides.

🪐

Saturn Has Rings

Saturn's rings are mainly made of ice and rocky particles.

🌌

Space is Enormous

The observable universe contains an enormous number of galaxies, each containing huge numbers of stars.

💫

Stars Have Different Sizes

Stars can range from relatively small stars to extremely large supergiants.

🕳️

Black Holes

A black hole has gravity so strong that light cannot escape from within its event horizon.

🚀

Space is Mostly Empty

The space between stars and planets contains extremely little matter compared with the air around us on Earth.

🌠

Shooting Stars

A "shooting star" is not actually a star. It is usually a small space rock burning up in Earth's atmosphere.

⏱️

Light Travels Fast

Light travels at about 300,000 km per second in vacuum.

👨‍🚀

Humans Have Walked on the Moon

Humans first walked on the Moon during Apollo 11 in 1969.
